About Monochrome

Monochrome is an art project combining writing and photography. It's also an experiment in running a digital project across multiple meaningful domain names — monochrome.photo for the gallery, monochrome.blog for long-form writing, and eventually monochrome.store for prints.

All photographs are shot in black and white — not converted, not filtered. The constraint is the point. Removing color forces you to see light, shadow, and structure.
