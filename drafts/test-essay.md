# The Impact of Social Media on Modern Communication

*Draft v3*

## Introduction

While social media has accelerated communication speed and expanded networks, it has fundamentally diminished the quality of human connection by prioritizing quantity over meaningful interection.. It is very popular and many people use it every day. In this essay, I will discuss how social media affects communication.

## Body Paragraph 1

One way social media affects communication is that it makes it faster. People can send messages instantly. Before social media, you had to write letters or make phone calls. Now you can just send a text or post something online.

## Body Paragraph 2

Another effect is that social media lets you talk to more people. You can have hundreds of friends on Facebook. You can follow thousands of people on Twitter. This means you can communicate with many people at once.

## Body Paragraph 3

However, social media also has some negative effects. Some people say it makes communication less personal. When you talk to someone online, you can't see their face or hear their voice. This might make it harder to really connect with people.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, social media has both positive and negative effects on communication. It makes communication faster and allows us to reach more people, but it might also make our interactions less personal. We should think about how we use social media in the future.
